# Latte LIVE: interactive pet design (for review before any video)

## 1. Who Latte is (the only things that never change)
- **Breed and look:** a long-haired cream kitten with soft light-brown forehead stripes, warm amber eyes, a small pink nose and fluffy tufted ears. Always the same size and face.
- **Attitude:** a confident, slightly dramatic little **diva** with expensive taste, sweet underneath. She judges you, then forgives you, then wants attention.
- **Not part of her identity:** outfits, sunglasses, hoodies, props. Each one belongs to a specific action or scene.
  - Her **default look is natural**: just her fluffy self, maybe a thin collar.
  - To keep outfits from leaking in, we make a new **clean identity reference** (her face and body, no clothes) and use it for every generation.

## 2. The stage: her room (the default scene)
One cozy room in soft evening light, built to give her things to do. She sits in the **lower middle** of the frame; the top half stays calm for the overlay.
- **In the room:** a fluffy bed, a vanity mirror, a little desk with a laptop and a mug, a feather wand toy, a plant, a window, and a pink neon "Latte" sign.

## 3. The golden rule: every clip starts and ends in the idle pose
We make one **anchor frame**: Latte sitting on her bed facing the camera in her natural look. It's the first and last frame of every in-room clip, so she always "returns home" without a visible cut.

Clips come in two kinds:

| Kind | How it returns to idle | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **In-room action** | Starts and ends on the anchor frame (use first and last keyframes where the model supports it; otherwise a 0.3s fade back) | Rose, GG, Overreact, idle behaviors |
| **Cutaway skit** | Different place or outfit, framed by a transition (sparkle wipe, flash) on the way in and out, so it doesn't need matching frames | Money Gun, Galaxy, Paris café, office |

## 4. Idle life: she's never just staring
- **Base loop (10s):** breathing, blinks, ear flicks and tail swish. Always playing underneath.
- **Every 15–30 seconds with no gifts,** she plays one idle behavior at random (each 4–6s, anchor to anchor):
  1. Grooms her paw and washes her face
  2. Bats at the feather wand dangling into frame
  3. Checks herself in the vanity mirror, then turns back satisfied
  4. Pushes the pen off the desk while staring at the camera
  5. Big dramatic yawn and stretch
  6. Leans toward the camera as if reading chat, head tilting
  7. Sips from her mug, gets a tiny milk mustache
- **Free reactions** (no gift needed, so non-gifters stay engaged):
  - "pspsps" or "Latte" in chat: she looks straight into the camera
  - Each like goal: a slow blink (cat for "I love you")
  - New follower: a little wave of the paw

## 5. Special gifts (what she does)

Gift names and coin values differ by region. Confirm them in your LIVE gift panel and we'll adjust the config.

| Gift | Tier | Latte's action | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rose** | 1 coin | Sniffs a rose that drops into frame, does a tiny sneeze, then tucks it behind her ear like a diva | In-room |
| **GG** | 1 coin | Gamer moment: a headset appears, she slams the keyboard, then throws her paws up in victory | In-room |
| **Overreact** (check exact name and value) | small | Soap-opera overreaction: dramatic zoom, jaw drop, paw to forehead, faints onto the pillow, peeks back up | In-room |
| **Money Gun** | 500 | **Boss mode:** gold chain and shades, she sprays cash from a money gun while bills rain down, then poses | Cutaway |
| **Galaxy** | 1000 | **Space trip:** a tiny astronaut helmet, floating among the stars with Earth behind her, a wobbly zero-gravity spin | Cutaway |
| *(optional)* Doughnut, Perfume | mid | Sends her to the **Paris café** or the **Cat Engineer office**: the scenes we already made, animated | Cutaway |
| *(optional)* Lion, Universe | top | **Runway show:** an outfit-change catwalk across several looks. The rarest, biggest moment | Cutaway |

**Rules that make gifting fun:**
- **Combos:** 10 Roses in a row gives her a whole bouquet, and the caption reads *"Latte has too many admirers"*.
- **Queue:** gifts play in order with the gifter's name on screen ("maria_23 made Latte overreact").
- **Big gifts jump the queue**, so a Galaxy plays next.
- **Latte's Favorite Human:** the top gifter's name and avatar sit next to her. Others can take the title.
- **Mood meter:** gifts and likes fill it. When it's full she does her happy "zoomies around the room" (an in-room clip) for everyone.

## 6. Sound
- Real recordings only: soft purr, tiny meows, a sneeze, keyboard clacks, cash register and cash rain, a whoosh for space.
- Each clip has its own short sound, plus a very quiet cozy lo-fi music bed under the room.
- No synthesized sounds.
