# Latte's idle: creative direction (nothing generated yet)

## The idea: "Waiting for you"
The idle isn't a cat sitting still. It's **Latte living her evening while she waits for her audience**, and the longer nobody gifts, the more dramatic she gets. Viewers should feel watched, charmed and a little guilty.

It's built in three layers:

### Layer 1: The living room (always on, 10s seamless loop)
The anchor frame comes alive. Nothing is frozen, and it should feel like a real camera left running in her room.

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| Latte | Calm breathing, two natural blinks, an ear twitch toward a sound off-screen, the tail tip curling slowly |
| Neon "Latte" sign | A very subtle flicker and buzz-glow |
| Window | Soft rain streaks down the glass; a few city lights twinkle |
| Mug | A thin wisp of steam rising |
| Vanity bulbs | Steady warm glow, with a slow light shift as if a car passed outside |
| Camera | **Locked off.** No movement at all, so every other clip can cut back to it invisibly |

**Prompt (image-to-video, first frame = last frame = anchor):**
> The kitten sits calmly on the bed breathing softly, blinks slowly twice, one ear twitches toward a sound off-screen, the tip of her tail curls lazily. A thin wisp of steam rises from the mug, soft rain streaks down the window, city lights twinkle, the pink neon sign glows with a faint flicker. Static locked-off camera, no camera movement, nothing else moves. Real footage, natural motion, photorealistic.

### Layer 2: Little moments (every 15–30s, random, never the same twice in a row)
Each one is 5 seconds and starts and ends on the anchor, so she's always back in place.

| # | Moment | Why viewers love it | Motion prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paw-wash** | The classic cute grooming shot | She lifts one front paw, licks it twice, wipes it over her ear and cheek, then sets the paw down and sits as before. |
| 2 | **Feather tease** (loop-safe) | Hunter mode; makes people laugh | A feather toy on a string dangles down into frame from above, held by an off-screen hand. Her eyes lock on, her butt wiggles, she swats and almost catches it, and the toy is pulled back up out of frame. She huffs and sits back in her original spot. The feather wand lying on the bed never moves. |
| 3 | **Mirror check** | Diva personality | She turns her head to look at herself in the vanity mirror, tilts her head approvingly, then turns back to the camera. |
| 4 | **The pen push** (loop-safe) | Peak cat comedy, and the off-screen owner becomes a running joke | Holding eye contact with the camera, she slowly pushes the pen off the desk edge. A beat later, an off-screen human hand places the pen back in exactly the same spot. She stares at the hand, unimpressed, and sits back as before. |
| 5 | **Big yawn** | Irresistible | A huge yawn showing tiny teeth and a curled tongue, a little shake of her head, then back to calm. |
| 6 | **Reading chat** | Makes viewers feel seen | She leans slightly toward the camera, eyes moving as if reading, tilts her head curiously, then settles back. |
| 7 | **Window watch** | Peaceful ASMR moment | She turns toward the window, watches the rain, her ears move, then she turns back to the camera. |

### Loop-safety rules (every clip must end exactly like the anchor)
Every object in the room is in one of three groups:

| Group | Objects | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Never touched** | bed, mirror, laptop, mug, plant, neon sign, window frame | They never move. Only Latte does. |
| **Naturally looping** | rain, steam, city lights, neon flicker, bulb glow | They're random textures, so they always match the anchor at the clip's first and last frame. |
| **Touched in a clip** | pen, feather wand, toys | Either the toy **comes in from off-screen and leaves again** (the feather on a string), or something on screen **puts it back** before the clip ends. The off-screen owner's hand does this. |

- **The off-screen owner** is a recurring character: your hand. It returns the pen, dangles the toy, and later offers treats on gifts. It fits the story, *"you're watching my cat"*.
- Every clip is generated with the **anchor as both the first and the last frame**, so the model has to land on the exact anchor.
- The boredom poses (Sigh, Drama) get their **own anchor images**. Their loops start and end on those images, and the transition clips connect anchor ↔ Sigh ↔ Drama.

### Layer 3: The boredom ladder (the money maker)
If **nobody gifts**, her mood escalates. This is the gentle "please send something" pressure, done with comedy, never begging.

| Time without a gift | Stage | What she does | Caption on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 min | **Hmm** | A long side-eye at the camera, then a slow blink of judgment | *"Latte is waiting…"* |
| 4 min | **Sigh** | Lies down, chin on her paws, big sad eyes looking up at the camera | *"Latte feels ignored 🥺"* |
| 6 min | **Drama** | Flops onto her side across the bed with a theatrical sigh, one paw over her face | *"Latte has fainted from lack of attention"* |

**Any gift instantly snaps her out of it.** She pops up alert and happy (a 2-second "perk up" clip back to the anchor) and plays the gift's action. Captions make the joke land and give viewers a clear reason to gift: *"save Latte from boredom"*.

The Sigh and Drama stages need their own short loops, because she stays in that pose until someone gifts:
- **Sigh loop:** lying chin on paws, slow blinks, an occasional sad glance up at the camera.
- **Drama loop:** flopped on her side, breathing, the tail flicking in annoyance, peeking out from under her paw every few seconds.

## Sound for idle
- A soft rain-on-window bed.
- A very quiet lo-fi chill track, low enough to sit under the stream.
- Small real sounds synced to moments: paw licks, a tiny yawn squeak, the pen clatter, a soft "mrrp" when she reads chat, a dramatic sigh on the flop.

## Rules that keep it looking real
- **Locked camera.** No zooms or pans in idle clips.
- **Slow, natural motion.** AI looks fake when it moves fast.
- **One action per clip.** Never chain two actions in one clip.
- **Same light in every clip:** the warm evening tone of the anchor.
- **Check every test at full size on a phone** before making the final.
