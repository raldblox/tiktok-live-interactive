# Your magical cat: character concepts and Runway prompts

**Direction:** a true four-legged cat, female, mythical, in game-cinematic 3D at legend-skin quality. She's an original character: we take the *quality level* of premium MOBA skins as the target, never their designs or names.

**Story:** you're streaming your magical cat. Viewers pet her, cast her skills, and open portals to the realms she wanders.

---

## Three concepts (make 4–8 images of each, then pick one)

### A. Lunaria, the Moonveil Cat (recommended)
- **Look:** long silver-white fur fading to soft lavender at the tips. Faint **constellation markings** glow along her back. A **floating crescent-moon circlet** hovers above her head. Her **tail flows like a veil of starry nebula mist**, twice her body length. Large **opal-violet eyes**, and a moonstone pendant on a fine silver chain.
- **Element:** moonlight and stars.
- **Palette:** silver, lavender, ice blue, with thin gold accents.
- **Why her:** she fits night, sleep and ASMR viewers, stands out on dark backgrounds, and feels premium and mysterious.

### B. Seraphyne, the Ember Lotus Cat
- **Look:** cream fur with rose-gold and coral tips. Her tail ends in **soft spirit-flame wisps that shed glowing petals**. A golden lotus ornament sits on her forehead, and she has warm amber eyes.
- **Element:** spirit fire and blossoms.
- **Palette:** cream, coral, rose gold, warm amber.
- **Why her:** the warmest and most "legend skin" in feel, with lots of flashy VFX.

### C. Mistral, the Jade Mist Cat
- **Look:** pale jade-white fur. **Mist flows from her paws and tail**, curling like water. She wears jade and pearl ornaments and has teal eyes with ripple reflections.
- **Element:** mist and water.
- **Palette:** jade, pearl, teal, silver.
- **Why her:** the calmest and most ASMR of the three, elegant and serene.

Use **9:16**. Generate 4–8 of each and pick by asking: *"Would I pay to see her?"*

### 2. Character sheet (consistency anchor), once you've picked

Use the winning image as the **reference**:
> character design sheet of the same magical cat, front view, three-quarter view, side view, sitting and standing poses, neutral dark background, consistent design

Every later image and video uses this sheet or the key visual as its reference, which keeps her looking the same.

### 3. Profile picture

Use the reference:
> close-up portrait of the same magical cat, looking into the camera, glowing eyes, magical particles, dark background, centered

Use **1:1**.

### 4. Her home: the default LIVE scene, 9:16

Use the reference:

Keep the top half calm, because the overlay panels sit there.

## What gifts do (the new game)

| Gift tier | Name on screen | What she does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 coin (Rose, Heart Me) | **Pet her** | Leans into an invisible hand and purrs. Small hearts and a soft chime. Cheap, constant and wholesome. |
| 5 (Finger Heart) | **Skill 1: Star Swipe** | A playful paw swipe that sends a trail of sparkles across the screen. |
| 20 (Perfume) | **Skill 2: Moon Leap** | An acrobatic leap through a ring of light and a graceful landing. |
| 30 (Doughnut) | **Skill 3: Constellation** | Draws the **gifter's name in stars** across the sky. Personal and screenshot-worthy. |
| 100–500 (Hand Hearts, Confetti, Money Gun) | **Portal** | Opens a portal; she walks through to a realm (a looping film scene) and wanders there a while. |
| 1000+ (Galaxy or bigger) | **ULTIMATE: Eclipse Ascension** | A full-screen transformation cinematic: she rises with wings of moonlight. The biggest moment of the stream. |

**What keeps viewers there:**
- **Mana bar:** likes and small gifts fill it. When it's full, she casts a **free ultimate for everyone**, which gets the whole chat cheering together.
- **Bond level:** it grows with every gift and **carries over between streams**. Each level unlocks a new form for her home scene, like Moonkit → Moonveil → Eclipse Empress, so regulars level her up like a skin collection.
- **Keeper of the Moon:** the top gifter's name glows in the stars above her until someone takes the title.
- **Chat:** "pspsps" makes her look into the camera. "pet" sends a floating heart.

## Sound overhaul

- **No synthesized sounds.** Use real recordings only: a soft purr, harp and celesta stingers for skills, a deep whoosh plus choir for portals, and an orchestral hit for the ultimate.
- **One calm music bed per realm**, an ambient or lo-fi loop, kept low under everything.
- Sources: royalty-free libraries with clear licenses, such as freesound.org filtered to CC0, or a paid library like Epidemic Sound or Artlist for music.

## Order of work (credits only after the design is right)

1. **Images:** concepts A, B and C, then pick a winner, then the character sheet, home scene and profile picture. These are cheap; iterate as much as you like.
2. **Video:**
   - home idle loop (10s)
   - Pet (5s)
   - Star Swipe, Moon Leap, Constellation (5s each)
   - portal walk-through (5s)
   - 2 realm loops (10s each)
   - Ultimate (10s)

   That's 9 clips. Test each at 5 seconds on the cheaper Turbo model first.
3. **Overlay:** I rebuild it around her: skill bar, mana, Bond level, the stars name effect, portal transitions, and the new sound.
